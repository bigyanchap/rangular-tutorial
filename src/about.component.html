About

Rangular Tutorial

Version 1.0.0. A step by step tutorial app that teaches Rangular: writing iOS and Android apps the Angular way.

Every screen is written in Angular style source and compiled by Rangular into React Native. If a lesson teaches it, this app uses it.

How the tutorial grows

Lessons follow the official Angular documentation, topic by topic. Each topic gets its Rangular equivalent: a short explanation, a Code block, a live demo, and a best practice. Lessons are added in batches of about twenty, and each batch is checked on a real phone before the next one starts.

Bookmarks and your place

Your bookmarks and the continue row on Home are saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. They keep across restarts, so the lesson you were reading is still your place when you come back.

Made for the stores

This app is being prepared for release on the App Store and Google Play. All 95 lessons are complete, and the icon, splash artwork, and store listing are prepared. The store accounts and the submission itself are the remaining steps.
